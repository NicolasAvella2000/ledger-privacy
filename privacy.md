# Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

**What it accesses.** Ledger personal requests only the Gmail read-only scope (`gmail.readonly`) for the owner's own Google account. It cannot send, delete, or modify email.

**What it does with the data.** It reads bank and payment notification emails from a short list of known senders and extracts transaction amounts, dates and categories into the owner's private finance ledger. Personal identifiers are removed before anything is stored.

**Sharing.** No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

**Storage and deletion.** Extracted data is kept in the owner's private storage. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's third-party access settings at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

**Users.** This app has a single user, its owner.
